A slice of history

From Naples to neighbourhood tables

Flatbreads topped with oil and herbs existed for centuries, but the pizza we recognise today took shape in Naples in the 18th and 19th centuries. Tomato, once newly introduced to Europe, met local dough and cheese to become an affordable, delicious street meal.

The classic Margherita combination of tomato, mozzarella and basil came to symbolise the colours of Italy. As pizza travelled, cooks made it local. In India, paneer, sweet corn, tandoori spices and chilli-forward sauces joined the tradition, creating pizzas meant for sharing.
